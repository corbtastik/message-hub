Sample body text. Lists can nest, which is worth checking at a bubble's width because the indent eats into an already narrow column:

- a first-level item
  - a second-level item under it
  - another one, long enough to wrap and show where the second line starts
- back out to the first level
  1. a numbered child
  2. a second numbered child
- a final first-level item

Two levels read comfortably. Three starts to look cramped at 334px, so a post that wants a deeper structure is usually better off with headings -- see the sample post from September 9th.
