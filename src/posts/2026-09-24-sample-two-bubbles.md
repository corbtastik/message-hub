Sample body text. A long message often arrives as two or three separate texts in a row. Separating chunks with `***` renders them as consecutive bubbles.

***

This is the second bubble. Only the last one in a run gets the tail and the avatar, which is how iOS draws consecutive messages from the same sender.

A list can live in the second bubble too:

- bubbles are split on the thematic break alone
- everything else about them is identical

> And so can a quotation.
