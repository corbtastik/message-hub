The kitchen chair has wobbled for two years. Yesterday I turned it over, found one loose dowel, and spent forty minutes with glue and a clamp. 🪑

It does not wobble. I have walked past it four times today just to sit on it. 😌

***

There is a specific satisfaction in a repair that nobody else will ever notice. Nothing about the room looks different. The chair is just quietly correct now.
