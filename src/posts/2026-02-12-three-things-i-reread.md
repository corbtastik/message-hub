Every so often I go back to something rather than starting something new. Three that reward it:

- 🌉 a short book on how bridges stand up, which I understand slightly more of each time
- ✉️ a collection of letters I first read far too young
- 📷 the manual for a camera I have owned for nine years

The third one is only half a joke. There are features in there I have never used.
