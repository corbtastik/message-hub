Blocked two hours in the middle of the day with nothing on a screen. Paper, a pen, one problem.

The first forty minutes were close to unbearable. The last twenty were the most useful of the week.

***

I do not think the lesson is that screens are the problem. I think it is that two uninterrupted hours is a rare thing and I had stopped noticing how rare.
